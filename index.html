Check what you paste into AI before it sends.

Dato is a Chrome extension for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It looks for keys, personal data and other confidential text on your device, then lets you edit, redact or stop the prompt.

Download Dato (ZIP) How to install

Free. Works in Chrome. It is not on the Chrome Web Store yet, so you install it from this ZIP in three steps.

Install in three steps

  1. 1

    Download the ZIP

    Save dato-extension.zip to your computer.

    Download Dato (ZIP)

  2. 2

    Extract it

    Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract all (Windows), or double-click it (Mac). You get a folder called extension. Keep it somewhere permanent: Chrome runs Dato from that folder.

  3. 3

    Load unpacked in Chrome

    Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked, and choose the extension folder.

Then refresh any ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini tab that was already open. Pin Dato from the puzzle-piece menu and open its popup to see the tab's status.

What Dato doesn't do

  • It is a guard rail, not a lock. It runs inside the browser, so a site change, another extension or the person at the keyboard can get around it. Pattern matching also misses things.
  • It checks typed and pasted text. File uploads are not inspected yet.
  • It stays on your device. Prompts are checked locally and never sent anywhere. The extension keeps only counts of what it found, never your text.
  • Chrome only, three sites. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. On any other site the popup says it is not protecting you.
