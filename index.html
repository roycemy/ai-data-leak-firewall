Install in Chromefree · local only

Download Dato for Chrome

  1. Unzip dato-extension.zip. You get a folder called extension.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.
  5. Pin Dato from the Extensions menu, then open ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and look for Dato · Protected locally.

Keep the folder on your computer. Chrome runs Dato from it.
